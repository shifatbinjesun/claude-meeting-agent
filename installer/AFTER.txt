CLAUMEET IS INSTALLED

Two quick steps are next (each takes about a minute, and you can skip either one):

  1. Claumeet's Google account
     Create a separate free Google account named like "Claumeet (AI)" and sign it in
     through Google's normal page. Claumeet never sees the password.

  2. A free Gemini API key
     Get one at https://aistudio.google.com/apikey (leave billing OFF) and paste it.
     It is checked, then stored only in Windows Credential Manager.

After that, the Claumeet Control Center opens in your browser. Paste a Google Meet
link and press Join. Anything you skipped is waiting in its setup checklist.
Start Claumeet any time from the Desktop shortcut "Claumeet Control Center".
