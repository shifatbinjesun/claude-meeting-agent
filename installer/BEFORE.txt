CLAUMEET - WHAT YOU NEED (all free)

Claumeet is an AI that joins your Google Meet and Zoom calls, openly says it is
an AI, and talks in the meeting's language (Bangla, English and many more).
It runs on THIS PC.

You will need:

1) A separate Google account for Claumeet  (free, 2 minutes)
   - This installer helps you create it and sign it in, right after the install.
   - Name it so people can see it is an AI, for example "Claumeet (AI)".
   - Do NOT use your personal account.

2) A free Gemini API key  (free, 1 minute)
   - The installer shows you where to get it (https://aistudio.google.com/apikey)
     and lets you paste it. Keep billing OFF: Claumeet only uses the free tier.

3) Google Chrome and Python 3.12
   - If either is missing, the installer installs it for you (official winget packages).

4) About 1 GB of free disk space and 8 GB of RAM.

What this installer does:
  - Installs only for your Windows user. No administrator rights, no drivers.
  - Downloads exact, checksum-verified library versions.
  - Adds a "Claumeet Control Center" shortcut and opens it when finished.
It never changes system settings, audio devices or security settings,
and it never joins a meeting by itself.
